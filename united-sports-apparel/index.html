Four steps, all with a real person in our store.

  1. 1

    Book a fitting

      Questions, answered

      Anything else? Call (269) 429-3194 or email sales@unitedsportsonline.com.

      What brand of letter jackets do you carry?

      We feature DeLong brand letter jackets with full quilt lining, and we keep a large inventory of them in our store.

      What sizes are available?

      All sizes from S to 6XL.

      Do I need an appointment for a letter jacket?

      Yes. Call or email us to make an appointment for a quote. Off-hours appointments are available.

      How much does a letter jacket cost?

      We offer great pricing on our letter jackets year round. The price depends on the details you choose, so call for details or request a quote by email.

      How do you decorate the jackets?

      With the finest quality chenille and embroidery, done in our shop.

      Is my school on your list?

      We make jackets for Lakeshore, St. Joe, Watervliet, Hartford, Bridgman, Coloma, Niles, Michigan Lutheran, Lakers, Eau Claire, River Valley, New Buffalo, Berrien Springs, Buchanan, Benton Harbor and more. If you don't see your school, give us a call.

      Do you do screen printing and embroidery for businesses and fundraisers?

      Yes. Screen printing and embroidery are done in-house for school fundraising and business promotional needs. Whether it's 6 garments or 3006, we can handle the job. Phone for no obligation pricing.

      How long does an order take?

      It depends on the order. Call for details.
